**Balloon Pop Game** is a simple, fun web game where players score points by clicking on a bouncing balloon before the timer runs out. The game features a visually engaging pink balloon, dynamic scoring, and a high score tracker—all within a clean, mobile-friendly design.

## 🎮 Features

- **Clickable Balloon:**  
  Pop the animated balloon as it moves randomly in the play area.
- **Scoring:**  
  Each click earns a point. Try to get the highest score in 10 seconds!
- **High Score Tracker:**  
  Track your best score each session.
- **Timer:**  
  A 10-second countdown challenges your reflexes.
- **Responsive Design:**  
  Looks great and works smoothly on desktop and mobile browsers.

## 🛡️ How It Works

1. Click **Start Game** to begin.
2. The pink balloon appears—click it as fast as possible!
3. Every click moves the balloon randomly; score increases by 1.
4. The timer counts down from 10 seconds.
5. When time’s up, see your score and if you’ve beaten your high score.
